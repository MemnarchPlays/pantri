Beef Lasagna

Serves 8
Prep time: 30 minutes
Cook time: 1 hour 15 minutes
Meal type: Dinner

Calories per serving: 520
Protein: 34g
Carbs: 45g
Fat: 22g

Ingredients:
3 boxes lasagna noodles
2 lbs ground beef
1 large onion, diced
4 cloves garlic, minced
2 jars (24 oz each) marinara sauce
16 oz ricotta cheese
2 cups shredded mozzarella
1/2 cup grated parmesan
2 eggs
2 tbsp olive oil
1 tsp Italian seasoning
Salt and pepper to taste

Instructions:
Cook lasagna noodles per box directions until al dente. Drain and lay flat.
Brown ground beef in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Drain fat.
Add onion and garlic to the beef and cook until softened, about 5 minutes.
Stir in marinara sauce and Italian seasoning. Simmer 10 minutes.
Mix ricotta, eggs, half the parmesan, salt and pepper in a bowl.
Preheat oven to 375 degrees F.
Spread a thin layer of meat sauce in the bottom of a 9x13 baking dish.
Layer: noodles, ricotta mixture, meat sauce, mozzarella. Repeat twice.
Top with remaining noodles, meat sauce, mozzarella, and parmesan.
Cover with foil and bake 45 minutes. Uncover and bake 15 more minutes until bubbly.
Rest 15 minutes before cutting.

Notes: Freezes well. Make a double batch and freeze half before baking.
